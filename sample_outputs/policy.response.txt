Sure! Here’s how returns work for electronics and accessories on ZentroMart:

* **Return window** – You have **7 calendar days from the delivery date** to return an electronic item. (If the item was delivered **before 1 August 2026**, the older policy gave a 10‑day window.)【RET-2】【RET-3】

* **Condition required** – The product must be **unused**, with its original tags, packaging, accessories and any free gifts. It must not be physically damaged, and any screen lock or account lock must be removed before you send it back【RET-4】.

* **Scope** – This applies to any electronics bought on ZentroMart, whether shipped by ZentroMart or a marketplace seller【RET-1】.

* **Return process** – A reverse‑pickup is arranged from your delivery address. There is **no return‑fee for electronics** (the ₹49 fee only applies to clothing, footwear and bags)【RET-6】.

* **After pickup** – The item goes through a quality check, and the refund is started within 2 business days (or at pickup for ZentroMart Plus members)【RET-7】.
